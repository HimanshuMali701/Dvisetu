Traditional Public Procurement Bottlenecks vs DviSetu Solution Architecture

🔴 Traditional Procurement Bottlenecks

  • 1. Lowest-Cost (L1) Tender Bias: Focus on cheapest cost and multi-year turnover history disqualifies early-stage startups with superior technology.
  • 2. Working Capital Liquidity Traps: Disconnected milestone sign-offs and slow manual bureaucracy trap startup cash flow during pilot execution.
  • 3. Absence of Empirical Scale Pathways: Successful pilots frequently stall without standardized frameworks to measure KPIs and authorize state rollouts.

🟢 The DviSetu Structured Solution

  • 1. Capability Matching & Automated Screening: Deterministic tag overlap matching combined with automated GSTIN, DPIIT, and certification verification.
  • 2. Milestone Disbursement Tracking: 20/30/30/20 tranche schedule digitally authorized upon verified technical deliverable submissions.
  • 3. Evidence-Based Scale Review Engine: Pilot baseline vs. actual KPI audit with third-party technical clearance for authorized state-wide expansion.
